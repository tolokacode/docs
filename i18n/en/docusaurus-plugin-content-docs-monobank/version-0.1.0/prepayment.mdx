# Cash on delivery with prepayment

Toloka extends WooCommerce's standard **Cash on delivery**. Shipping plugins keep working with it as before.

Open **WooCommerce → Settings → Payments → Cash on delivery**, section **Prepayment through plata by mono**.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Prepayment | A **fixed amount** in UAH or a **percentage** of the order. |
| Prepayment size | `0` means regular cash on delivery without prepayment. |
| plata by mono token | Leave empty to use the token from the official "plata by mono" plugin. |

While the prepayment is 0, nothing changes. So installing the plugin doesn't affect your shop on its own.

## How it works

1. The customer chooses cash on delivery and goes to the monobank payment page.
2. After paying, the order moves to **Processing**.
3. The amount the customer pays on delivery is shown in the order in the **"Cash on delivery (waybill)"** row.

If the customer didn't pay, the order stays **Pending payment**, and the thank-you page has a "Pay the prepayment" button.
